Urgent Care & Acute Illnesses

Feeling under the weather? We're here to help you feel better, faster.

From coughs, colds, flu, and fevers to ear infections, sore throats, and other sudden illnesses, we provide prompt, compassionate care for the whole family—no appointment needed.

what we can help with
  • Cold, cough and flu-like illness
  • Sore throat and strep throat
  • Ear infections and sinus infections
  • Fever and body aches
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea
  • Allergic reactions (mild to moderate)
  • Asthma flare-ups (mild)
  • Pink eye and eye irritation
  • Rashes and insect bites
  • Ear irrigations/wax removal

Ready to be seen? Walk in or call today.
