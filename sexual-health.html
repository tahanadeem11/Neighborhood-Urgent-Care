Confidential, respectful sexual health care

Your health. Your privacy. Our priority.

We provide confidential, professional sexual health services, including STD/STI screening, urine testing and vaginal swab collection. Our team offers discreet evaluations, clear guidance and personalized treatment to help you stay informed, healthy and confident.

Getting tested is a responsible and caring step for you and your partners, and you never need to feel embarrassed about it. At Neighborhood Urgent Care in Columbus, Ohio, you will be treated with respect and without judgment, and your questions are always welcome.

Many infections do not cause obvious symptoms, so screening is the only way to know for sure. Walk in any day of the week, explain your concerns, and your provider will recommend the right tests and next steps.

Visit us when you have

  • You are sexually active and want peace of mind
  • A new partner or a partner who has tested positive
  • Burning, itching, discharge or unusual symptoms
  • You need a pregnancy test
  • You have questions about sexual health and want clear answers

Call 911 or go to the ER for

  • Severe pelvic or abdominal pain, especially with fever
  • Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding
  • Trouble breathing or chest pain
  • Fainting or loss of consciousness
  • You need emergency care after a sexual assault

FAQ

Sexual Health & STD/STI Testing: Common Questions

Is STD/STI testing confidential?

Privacy is a priority for our team. Your visit is handled discreetly and with respect.

What does testing involve?

Depending on your situation, testing may use a urine sample or a vaginal swab collection. Your provider will explain what to expect before anything is done.

Do I need to wait for symptoms before getting tested?

No. Many infections cause no symptoms at all. If you are sexually active and want peace of mind, screening is a good idea.

Do you offer pregnancy testing?

Yes. We offer pregnancy testing and can guide you on next steps.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome seven days a week. Call +1 (614) 394 8045 if you have questions before you come in.
