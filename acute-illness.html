Fast, walk-in care when you suddenly feel sick

Feeling under the weather? We are here to help you feel better, faster.

From coughs, colds, flu and fevers to ear infections, sore throats and other sudden illnesses, we provide prompt, compassionate care for the whole family, with no appointment needed.

Getting sick rarely happens at a convenient time. At Neighborhood Urgent Care in Columbus, Ohio, you can walk in seven days a week, be seen by a caring provider, and leave with a clear plan to start feeling better, without waiting days for a primary care appointment or sitting in a crowded emergency room.

Your provider will listen to your symptoms, examine you, use on-site rapid testing when it helps, and explain your diagnosis and treatment options in plain language so you know exactly what to do next.

Illnesses we can help with

Cold, cough and flu-like illness

Evaluation and relief-focused care for congestion, cough, body aches and seasonal viruses.

Sore throat and strep throat

A throat exam and rapid strep testing so treatment can start promptly if it is needed.

Ear infections and sinus infections

Assessment of ear pain, pressure, congestion and facial discomfort in children and adults.

Fever and body aches

We look for the cause of your fever and guide you on treatment and recovery at home.

Urinary tract infections

Evaluation and treatment for burning, urgency and frequent urination.

Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea

Assessment and practical guidance to help you stay hydrated and recover.

Allergic reactions (mild to moderate)

Evaluation and treatment of hives, itching and other mild to moderate allergy symptoms.

Asthma flare-ups (mild)

Care for mild wheezing and cough flare-ups, with guidance on what to do next.

Pink eye and eye irritation

Examination of red, itchy or irritated eyes and treatment recommendations.

Rashes and insect bites

Evaluation of rashes, bites and stings, and relief for itching and swelling.

Ear irrigations / wax removal

Gentle ear irrigation to clear built-up wax and relieve muffled hearing.

Visit us when you have

  • Symptoms that came on quickly and need attention today
  • Your regular doctor cannot see you soon enough
  • Fever, sore throat, cough, ear pain or urinary discomfort
  • Mild to moderate allergic reactions or asthma flare-ups
  • A sick child outside normal office hours

Call 911 or go to the ER for

  • Trouble breathing or shortness of breath at rest
  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Signs of a stroke: face drooping, weakness, confusion or trouble speaking
  • Severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face, lips or throat
  • Loss of consciousness or a seizure

FAQ

Acute Illness: Common Questions

Which illnesses do you treat?

We treat common sudden illnesses such as colds, flu, fever, sore throat, ear and sinus infections, urinary tract infections, mild allergic reactions, pink eye, rashes and more. If something needs a higher level of care, we will tell you and point you to the right place.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We provide care for the entire family, from kids to adults.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome seven days a week. You can also call +1 (614) 394 8045 with questions before you come in.

Should I come in if I have a fever?

A provider can evaluate a fever and look for its cause. If a fever comes with trouble breathing, chest pain, confusion or a stiff neck, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept Medicaid, Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans. Call us to confirm your specific plan.
