Who We Are

A Neighborhood Clinic Built On Trust.

Neighborhood Urgent Care was created to give families in northeast Columbus fast, caring treatment close to home. We are located at 5957 Cleveland Ave. Columbus OH 43231, and our doors are open to everyone.

We know that good care is more than a prescription. It is about feeling heard, respected and comfortable. That is why our team takes time to listen, explains each step clearly, and honors the faith, language and privacy preferences of our patients, including our Muslim neighbors.

  • Same-day walk-in visits
  • Care for all ages
  • Modesty and privacy respected
  • Rapid testing on site
  • Referrals when you need a specialist
  • Friendly, professional staff
Our Values

What Guides Our Care.
