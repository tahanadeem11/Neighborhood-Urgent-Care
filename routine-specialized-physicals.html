Physical exams and paperwork, done quickly and accurately

School, sports, work, T8 or DOT: we've got you covered!

From routine checkups to specialized physical exams, we make it quick, easy and convenient to get the care and paperwork you need.

Whether your child needs a physical before the school year, you are joining a sports team, starting a new job, or need a DOT or T8 physical, you can walk in to Neighborhood Urgent Care in Columbus, Ohio and be seen without scheduling days or weeks ahead.

Our providers complete a thorough exam, fill out your forms accurately, and answer your questions so you can check this item off your list and get on with your day.

Physicals we offer

DOT physicals

Physical exams for drivers who need DOT medical paperwork, done quickly and accurately.

T8 physicals

T8 physical exams completed quickly, with your paperwork taken care of.

School and daycare physicals

Physical exams and forms for school and daycare enrollment.

Sports physicals

Pre-participation exams so young athletes and adults can play with confidence.

Pre-employment physicals

Exams and paperwork required by employers before you start a new job.

Camp and activity forms

Health forms for summer camps, clubs and other activities completed during your visit.

Visit us when you have

  • School, daycare or camp requires a physical
  • You are joining a sports team or activity
  • A new job requires a pre-employment exam
  • You need a DOT or T8 physical
  • It has been a while since your last checkup

Call 911 or go to the ER for

  • Trouble breathing or shortness of breath at rest
  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Signs of a stroke: face drooping, weakness, confusion or trouble speaking
  • Severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face, lips or throat
  • Loss of consciousness or a seizure

FAQ

Routine & Specialized Physicals: Common Questions

Do I need an appointment for a physical?

No. Walk-ins are welcome seven days a week, and you can call +1 (614) 394 8045 if you have questions before you come in.

What should I bring to my physical?

Bring any forms to be completed, a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of medications and allergies, and vaccine records for school or daycare physicals.

Do you do physicals for children?

Yes. We offer school, daycare, sports and camp physicals for children and teens, as well as exams for adults.

Can you complete DOT and T8 physicals?

Yes. We offer DOT and T8 physicals. Call us if you have questions about what to bring.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept Medicaid, Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans. Call us to confirm your plan and any coverage for your specific type of physical.
