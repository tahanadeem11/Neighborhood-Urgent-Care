FAQ

Questions? We Have Answers.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome, and you can also call us at 614-394-8045 with any questions before you come in.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM, and Saturday and Sunday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We provide care for the whole family, from children to adults.

Do you offer physicals?

Yes. We offer school, sports, work, T8 and DOT physicals.

What if I have a life-threatening emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
